# LINGUAWORLD — ORIGINAL CHARACTER CONCEPT ART

## Main Prompt

Create an original, premium-quality 2D anime chibi fantasy RPG character for a cozy language-learning adventure game called LinguaWorld.

### Art Direction
- Anime Chibi Fantasy.
- Medium detail level.
- Soft dark-brown outlines, never harsh pure-black outlines.
- Warm, cozy, whimsical fantasy atmosphere.
- Beautiful, clean 2D digital illustration.
- Soft cel shading with 2–3 levels of shading.
- Gentle ambient lighting and subtle highlights.
- Carefully balanced pastel and earthy colors.
- Professional indie RPG character design quality.
- Cute, expressive, memorable and visually appealing.

### Character Identity
Design an original, gender-neutral young adventurer named Luma.

Luma is a curious, friendly and optimistic little wanderer exploring a magical world through language learning.

The character should feel:
- Approachable and emotionally expressive.
- Curious and adventurous.
- Warm, comforting and charming.
- Suitable for a cozy fantasy RPG.
- Timeless rather than tied to a temporary fashion trend.

Do not make Luma look like a warrior, knight, or heavily armored fighter.

### Character Proportions
- Approximately 2.5–3 heads tall.
- Large expressive head.
- Compact but clearly readable torso.
- Short, natural arms and legs.
- Cute but balanced body proportions.
- Clear silhouette at small display sizes.

### Face Design
- Beautiful anime-style eyes with detailed iris, pupil and multiple subtle highlights.
- Soft eyebrows capable of expressive changes.
- Small nose.
- Small flexible mouth.
- Gentle blush on cheeks.
- Friendly, curious neutral expression.
- Face must be clean, symmetrical and readable.

### Hair Design
- Original, recognizable hairstyle.
- Multiple clearly separated hair clumps.
- Natural layered hair silhouette.
- Hair should be suitable for modular customization.
- Front hair, side hair and back hair must be distinguishable.
- Avoid extremely thin strands and excessive tiny details.

### Base Outfit
Create a simple but memorable fantasy adventurer outfit:
- Comfortable tunic or short fantasy jacket.
- Simple belt with a small decorative detail.
- Soft boots or adventure shoes.
- A small symbolic detail suggesting curiosity, exploration or learning.
- Optional tiny satchel, provided it can be separated as an equipment attachment.

Use warm cream, muted blue, sage green and soft golden accents.

The base outfit should be visually appealing but not overly elaborate. It must leave room for future equipment customization.

### Modular Character Design Requirements
The character must be designed with future Spine 2D skeletal animation and equipment customization in mind.

Make these parts visually separable:
1. Body.
2. Head.
3. Face.
4. Back hair.
5. Front hair.
6. Hat area.
7. Outfit.
8. Left arm.
9. Right arm.
10. Left leg.
11. Right leg.
12. Shoes.
13. Front accessories.
14. Back accessories.

Keep attachment boundaries and overlap areas clean.

Do not merge hair into the head.
Do not merge hats into hair.
Do not merge accessories into clothing.
Do not let the outfit obscure the basic body structure in a way that makes future clothing replacement difficult.

### Output Composition
Create a professional character concept presentation sheet containing:

1. One large full-body FRONT VIEW as the main focus.
2. One smaller SIDE VIEW.
3. One smaller BACK VIEW.
4. A small facial expression strip with:
   - Neutral.
   - Happy.
   - Curious.
   - Surprised.
   - Sleepy.
   - Proud.
5. A compact color palette.
6. Small visual callouts identifying the modular equipment areas.

The three body views must represent exactly the same character, hairstyle, proportions and base outfit.

Front view must be standing upright in a neutral relaxed pose:
- Arms slightly away from the torso.
- Legs naturally separated.
- Hands clearly visible.
- Feet fully visible.
- No dramatic perspective.
- No props blocking the body.

Use a clean warm off-white background.
Keep the character centered and unobstructed.
Make the concept sheet look like professional production art for a real 2D RPG.

### Technical Art Constraints
- Clean silhouette.
- Consistent line weight.
- Clear separation between body parts.
- No unnecessary micro-details.
- No complicated lighting effects.
- No 3D rendering.
- No photorealism.
- No pixel art.
- No UI mockup.
- No environment illustration.
- No text labels covering the character.

The final result must be suitable as the visual master reference for a modular 2D character production pipeline.

## Negative Prompt

pixel art, 3D model, photorealistic, realistic human anatomy, harsh black outlines, extremely thick outlines, flat generic vector art, overly simplistic sticker design, excessive gradients, excessive texture, overly detailed armor, dark horror fantasy, sci-fi outfit, huge weapons, extreme perspective, dynamic action pose, cropped feet, cropped head, extra limbs, malformed hands, fused fingers, asymmetrical eyes, inconsistent character proportions, different characters across views, merged accessories, cluttered background, text watermark, logo, UI elements.
